UNIT INTRODUCTION (Write 6–8 lines) 
The entrepreneurial ecosystem refers to the network of individuals, organizations, 
institutions, and policies that collectively support entrepreneurship and startup development. 
It includes entrepreneurs, investors, incubators, accelerators, government bodies, educational 
institutions, financial institutions, and markets. A strong ecosystem reduces business risk, 
promotes innovation, provides access to capital and mentorship, and accelerates economic 
growth. Countries with well-developed ecosystems experience higher levels of employment, 
innovation, and competitiveness.
